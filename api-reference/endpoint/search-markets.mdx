---
title: "Search Markets"
openapi: "GET /api/v1/markets/search"
---

Search Polymarket and Kalshi markets by title keyword. Use this endpoint when you know the topic and need a `condition_id` for another endpoint.

## Read the results

- `q` is required. It must be 1 to 512 characters before whitespace trim and non-empty after trim.
- Each item carries `id` (the `mkt_` market ID), `condition_id` (the raw provider ID), `title`, `slug`, `category`, `platform`, and `status`.
- `status` is `active` or `closed`. The `status` filter accepts `active`, `closed`, or `all` (default).
- `category` filters by market category.
- The cursor advances over grouped market results, not raw sub-market rows. `limit` is 1 to 100 with a default of 20.
- `total` is present only when the read model exposes a count.

```bash
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $OXINSIDER_API_KEY" \
  "https://api.0xinsider.com/api/v1/markets/search?q=bitcoin&status=active&limit=20"
```

Pass `condition_id` to [Get Market Intel](/api-reference/endpoint/get-market-intel) or [Get Market Snapshot](/api-reference/endpoint/get-market-snapshot). Pass raw `condition_id` values, never `mkt_` IDs, to [Batch Get Market Intel](/api-reference/endpoint/batch-get-market-intel).
