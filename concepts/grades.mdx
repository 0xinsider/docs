A grade is one letter, `S` to `F`, on each tracked Polymarket wallet. Use it to filter traders, trades, and positions before you read anything else.

## Read the scale

| Grade | `score` band | On the leaderboard |
|---|---|---|
| `S` | 99.7 to 100 | Yes |
| `A` | 98.2 to 99.7 | Yes |
| `B` | 90 to 98.2 | Yes |
| `C` | 55 to 90 | No |
| `D` | 15 to 55 | No |
| `F` | 0 to 15 | No |

- `score` is the cohort percentile of the composite, 0 to 100, clamped inside the grade band.
- A trader never sorts across a grade boundary. Sort by `score` inside one grade.
- `grade` and `score` are absent for a trader with fewer than 5 markets.
- The grade is relative to the cohort. It moves when the cohort moves.
- Kalshi prints are anonymous and carry no grade. See [Platforms](/concepts/platforms).

## Know what sets the grade

The composite is `0.95 x realized P&L percentile + 0.05 x skill blend`.

- Realized P&L is the all-time total P&L minus open-position P&L. Its cohort percentile carries 95 percent of the composite.
- The skill blend is a tie-breaker between traders with near-identical realized P&L. Its inputs and weights: calibration edge 0.27, resolved-market track record 0.22, max drawdown 0.16, equity smoothness 0.14, win rate 0.13, Sortino 0.08.
- Each input is shrunk for sample size, then ranked inside the cohort.

Guardrails cap a band assignment:

- A grade above `C` needs net-positive realized P&L.
- `B` needs 10 or more resolved markets.
- `A` needs 50 or more resolved markets, $20,000 or more volume, calibration data, and a max drawdown at or under 60 percent.
- `S` needs 200 or more resolved markets, $50,000 or more volume, positive edge, and a max drawdown at or under 60 percent.
- A trader idle for more than 365 days is capped at `C`.

Promotion floors lift a proven winner that the percentile under-ranks. Each floor needs positive edge, profit, a max drawdown at or under 60 percent, and recent activity:

- 1,000 or more resolved markets and $20,000 or more volume guarantee at least `A`.
- 1,000 or more resolved markets and $1,000,000 or more realized P&L guarantee `S`.
- 200 or more resolved markets and a composite at or above the `C` band unlock the P&L ladder. Realized P&L of $1,000,000 gives `S`, $250,000 gives `A`, and $100,000 gives `B`.

## Read a graded trader

```json
{
  "object": "trader",
  "data": {
    "id": "trd_0x204f72f35326db932158cba6adff0b9a1da95e14",
    "address": "0x204f72f35326db932158cba6adff0b9a1da95e14",
    "username": "swisstony",
    "grade": "S",
    "score": 99.84,
    "streak_tier": "hot",
    "rank": 3,
    "pnl": { "total": 10412880.51, "realized": 10034112.23, "unrealized": 378768.28 },
    "stats": { "markets_traded": 4120, "win_rate": 0.6231, "daily_win_rate": 0.5714, "total_volume": 48211004.11 },
    "sync_status": "synced"
  }
}
```

- `pnl.realized` is the number behind the grade. `pnl.total` includes open positions.
- `streak_tier` is recent form on a separate axis. See [Signal scoring](/concepts/signal-scoring).
- Every `pnl` and `stats` value is nullable. Do not read `null` as zero.

## Filter by grade

`min_grade` is an allowlist. `min_grade=A` returns `S` and `A`. `min_grade=B` returns `S`, `A`, and `B`.

| Endpoint | `min_grade` values | Default |
|---|---|---|
| [`/whale-trades`](/api-reference/endpoint/get-whale-trades), [`/whale-trades/history`](/api-reference/endpoint/get-whale-trades-history) | `S` to `F` | none |
| [`/positions`](/api-reference/endpoint/get-positions), [`/large-positions`](/api-reference/endpoint/list-large-positions) | `S` to `F` | none |
| [`/markets/smart-money-flows`](/api-reference/endpoint/smart-money-flows) | `S` to `F` | `B` |
| [`/sports-edge-signals`](/api-reference/endpoint/get-sports-edge-signals) | `S`, `A`, `B` only | `B` |

```bash
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $OXINSIDER_API_KEY" \
  "https://api.0xinsider.com/api/v1/whale-trades?min_grade=A&category=crypto&limit=50"
```

The leaderboard has no `min_grade`. It returns `S`, `A`, and `B` traders only.

## Handle a null strategy

A leaderboard entry carries `strategy_type`. It is `null` when no classification exists. Keep the trader unless your workflow needs the label. See [Strategy types](/concepts/strategy-types).
